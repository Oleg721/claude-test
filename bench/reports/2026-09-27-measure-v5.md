# Measure v5 — the mask gate removed, sixteen stills (2026-09-27)

Prompt v5 (`health/docs/nutrition/portion-prompts-v5.md`): the model no longer answers `maskFitsItem`; it names the item and picks the product, state and shape rows, and the server multiplies. Every calibration still so far: the two of 2026-09-23/24 (apple 314 g, banana 157 g) and the fourteen of 2026-09-27 (a 320 g green apple at 12° / 47° / 61°, a 127 g red apple at 48°, a 141 g banana in three positions, mandarins of 68 g and 49 g, apples of 242 g and 113 g at ~42° and from above). Opus, Sonnet and Haiku at effort low, thinking off, over the Agent SDK; 48 calls, all ok. The HEIC stills were converted with `sips` before the run: the bench converts each still once per config in parallel and the three renames collide (Error 13), so the JPEGs were placed in the run's `jpeg/` folder first and reused.

What it shows: with the gate gone the three models get the same grams on every still where they name the item right — the numbers are the server's geometry alone. Sonnet named all sixteen right: MAPE 9 % over sixteen, 6 % without the 61° still, where the LiDAR loses the far side (−52 %, still *medium*: the server rule for broken measurements is open). The misses are names only. Haiku called the 49 g mandarin a lemon at both angles (`other`, no grams) and got the 68 g one right this time; Opus called the 242 g apple from the stem end a pear again (271 g, +12 %) and the 49 g mandarin from above a yellow plum (50 g at 1.0, right by accident). The 314 g apple now reads 341 g (+9 %) at the sphere factor 0.94; on the ten round stills that set the factor it is the outlier. Cost per call: Haiku $0.006; Sonnet $0.011, $0.08 on the two calls in sixteen that needed a structured-output retry; Opus $0.03, up to $0.23 with a retry (four in sixteen). The visual guesses stay 130–200 g for every apple from 113 g to 320 g.
